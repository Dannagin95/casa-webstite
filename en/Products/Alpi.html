FAQ

What is the key difference between engineered wood flooring and solid wood flooring?

Solid wood flooring is cut from a single piece of timber and can therefore be more susceptible to expansion, contraction and warping as conditions change. By contrast, engineered wood flooring uses a multi-layer construction in which the natural wood surface is stabilised by cross-layered Birch Plywood beneath, helping control dimensional movement while preserving the authentic character of real wood.

How should plank format and dimensions be selected?

Plank dimensions have a direct influence on how a space is perceived. At CASA, we recommend considering the following factors:

Spatial Proportion: Larger spaces can accommodate wider boards, reducing the number of visible joints and reinforcing a more expansive sense of scale. Smaller spaces often benefit from more moderate board widths for a balanced composition.

Visual Effect: Laying boards along the length of a room or in the direction of natural light can visually extend the space and create a greater sense of depth.

Personal Aesthetic: You may choose a traditional straight-lay pattern for a restrained expression, or a more graphic pattern such as Herringbone to create a distinctive architectural rhythm.

What determines the quality of wood flooring installation?

Installation quality depends on workmanship, subfloor flatness and the use of appropriate installation materials such as adhesives and underlay. CASA Parquet follows a process designed to support professional installation standards.

Does CASA Parquet provide on-site assessment?

Yes. CASA’s technical team can visit the site to measure the space, check subfloor moisture and advise on suitable product specifications.

What is the warranty policy?

CASA provides a 12-month warranty for installation-related issues and a 10–25 year product warranty depending on the flooring line. Reported issues are handled within 48 hours. Learn more about our warranty policy from CASA.

What does dimensional stability mean, and what role does Birch Plywood play?

Dimensional stability is measured in laboratory conditions according to UNI EN 1910. By using a multi-layer Birch Plywood core with a dedicated vinylic adhesive system, engineered wood flooring CASA flooring is designed to resist bending forces and maintain structural stability as temperature and humidity conditions change.

Can CASA wood flooring be installed over underfloor heating or cooling systems?

Yes. The source specifies CASA flooring as suitable for underfloor heating and cooling systems, supported by the natural thermal behaviour and dimensional performance of the engineered construction.

Can wood flooring be installed in higher-humidity areas such as kitchens or bathrooms?

The source specifies that kitchens are suitable where the protective surface finish is maintained. In dry bathroom zones, installation is possible when the appropriate process wall-edge moisture protection and specialised joint waterproofing procedures are followed.

What are the advantages of CASA’s oil finish?

The oil finish penetrates into the wood fibres rather than forming a plastic-like film over the surface, helping preserve a more natural tactile quality. The product is stated to meet French VOC Label Class A+ for indoor-air safety and allows localised restoration and maintenance.

Do CASA adhesives and surface finishes contain formaldehyde?

The source states that CASA does not use adhesives or surface coatings containing formaldehyde and that its products meet European Class A+ requirements for very low emissions.

What is the difference between certifications and test reports?

Technical test reports document measured physical and mechanical properties such as hardness, abrasion resistance and water resistance. Certifications such as CO/CQ, FSC and CARB P2 relate to origin, compliance or responsible sourcing requirements.

How should oil-finished wood flooring be cleaned and maintained?

Remove dust regularly and clean with a well-wrung damp cloth together with a suitable neutral cleaning solution. Avoid leaving excess water on the surface, as mineral residue can affect the appearance of the protective oil finish.

Why can CASA natural wood flooring develop more character over time?

With an oil-finished surface and textured wood treatment, everyday use can gradually develop a natural patina, giving the material a deeper and warmer appearance over time.
