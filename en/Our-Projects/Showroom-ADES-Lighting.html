Showroom ADES Lighting

CASA Parquet worked alongside the project to complete the engineered wood flooring for a lighting showroom defined by a contemporary architectural language.

ADES Lighting works in lighting solutions, with collections and lighting systems for residential, commercial and architectural spaces. The showroom is conceived as an experiential environment where light can be perceived in real application settings.

The engineered wood flooring by CASA Parquet was selected in a straight-lay format with a naturally light wood tone, creating a continuous material foundation that allows light to remain the primary focus throughout the showroom.

Each display area presents a different approach to lighting, from living environments to architectural accents. Across these settings, visitors can directly observe the effect of each lighting solution.

Within this composition, the wood flooring by CASA Parquet acts as a continuous foundation, connecting the different areas through a consistent material language and helping the showroom remain coherent so the lighting solutions can be experienced more clearly.

Showroom ADES Lighting demonstrates the value of a space shaped by the relationship between light, material and architecture. When each element is given a clear role, the experience becomes natural, balanced and layered.

Every detail in the project is selected to fulfil its role, contributing to a considered, balanced interior with a strong sense of depth.
