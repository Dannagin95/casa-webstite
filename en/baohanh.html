Warranty Guide

At CASA Parquet, we stand behind the quality and finish of our products. The warranty covers manufacturing-related technical defects for one year from the date of handover.

Warranty Coverage

CASA Parquet's warranty applies to technical defects arising from materials, manufacturing, or installation, including finished work under normal conditions of use.

Exclusions

The warranty does not cover damage caused by impact, external factors, misuse, natural wear over time, improper storage, insufficient maintenance, or unauthorized intervention or modification.

Repair & Support

CASA Parquet currently does not provide standalone repair services. We can, however, recommend appropriate solutions to maintain floor quality and extend surface life. Please send clear images of the issue so our team can respond with guidance and suitable recommendations.

How to Submit a Warranty Claim

If a warranty issue arises, please submit your full name, order information or handover record, the relevant product or installation area, and a detailed description of the issue.

For a more accurate assessment, please attach both overview and close-up images of the affected area.

Once the information has been received and verified, CASA Parquet will contact you to agree on an appropriate resolution under the current warranty policy.

Warranty Period & Conditions

  • Warranty claims must be submitted within 12 months.
