Handcrafted Wood Flooring Surfaces

Wood flooring surfaces at CASA are finished through meticulous handcrafted processes that combine material expertise with a design approach suited to contemporary architecture. Each grain is carefully selected and treated to preserve its natural character while adding visual depth and a sense of stability to the living space.

Every plank carries the marks of time.

Natural Brushed

A light brushing process removes the softer wood fibers, revealing the material's natural texture and resilience.

Extra Brushed

Repeated, controlled brushing removes the softest fibers to reveal more pronounced grain and greater surface depth.

Pre-Smoothed

The surface is refined through ultra-fine sanding, creating a soft tactile feel and a clean, even appearance.

Hand-Sawn

Traditional hand-sawing creates irregular marks in depth and direction, giving the surface a character that cannot be mechanically repeated.

Hand-Rounded Edges

Edges are rounded by hand, gently softening the profile for a restrained, classically informed finish.

FAQ

What Is the Core Difference Between Engineered Wood Flooring and Solid Wood Flooring?

Solid wood flooring is cut from a single piece of timber and is therefore more susceptible to seasonal expansion, contraction, and warping. By contrast, engineered wood flooring uses a layered engineered structure: a natural wood wear layer is stabilized by cross-laminated Birch Plywood, helping control dimensional movement while preserving the authentic character of real wood.

How Should Plank Format and Dimensions Be Selected?

Plank dimensions directly affect spatial perception. CASA recommends considering the following factors:

Spatial proportion: Larger spaces can accommodate wider planks to reduce visual joints and reinforce scale. Smaller rooms often benefit from medium-width planks for a more balanced composition.

Visual effect: Running planks along the room's length or in the direction of natural light can visually extend the space and enhance a sense of depth.

Personal aesthetic: You may choose a traditional straight lay for a restrained expression, or a more graphic pattern such as Herringbone to create a stronger architectural accent.

What Determines the Quality of a Wood Flooring Installation?

Installation quality depends on workmanship, subfloor flatness, and the use of appropriate installation materials such as adhesives and underlay. CASA Parquet follows a process professional installation standard.

Does CASA Parquet Provide On-Site Surveys?

Yes. Our technical team can visit the site to take measurements, assess subfloor moisture, and recommend suitable flooring options for the space.

What Does the Warranty Cover?

We provide a 12-month warranty for installation-related issues and a 10–25 year product warranty depending on the flooring range. Reported issues are handled within 48 hours. See the warranty policy of CASA.

What Does Dimensional Stability Mean, and What Role Does Birch Plywood Play?

Dimensional stability is measured in laboratory conditions according to UNI EN 1910. By using a multilayer Birch Plywood core with a dedicated vinylic adhesive system, engineered wood flooring CASA flooring maintains strong bending resistance and structural stability under changes in temperature and humidity.

Can CASA Wood Flooring Be Installed Over Underfloor Heating or Cooling?

Yes. With even heat transfer, natural flexibility, and the thermal behavior of wood, CASA flooring can work with underfloor systems while maintaining comfort and dimensional performance.

Can Wood Flooring Be Installed in Higher-Humidity Areas Such as Kitchens or Bathrooms?

Kitchen installations are suitable when the protective surface finish is properly maintained. In dry bathroom zones, flooring may be installed when the correct process moisture-control process is followed at wall edges and joints.

What Are the Advantages of CASA's Oil Finish?

An oil finish penetrates into the wood fibers rather than forming a plastic-like film, preserving a more natural tactile quality. The product meets French VOC Label Class A+ for indoor air quality and also allows localized maintenance and restoration.

Do CASA Adhesives or Surface Finishes Contain Formaldehyde?

CASA states that its adhesives and surface coatings do not use added formaldehyde. Products are specified to meet European Class A+ low-emission requirements for indoor air quality.

What Is the Difference Between Certifications and Test Reports?

Technical test reports document measured performance such as hardness, abrasion resistance, and water resistance, while certifications such as CO/CQ, FSC, and CARB P2 relate to origin, compliance, or sourcing requirements.

How Should Oil-Finished Wood Flooring Be Cleaned and Maintained?

Remove dust regularly and clean with a well-wrung damp cloth and a suitable neutral cleaner. Avoid excessive plain water, as mineral residue can dull the protective oil finish.

Why Can CASA Natural Wood Flooring Develop More Character Over Time?

With an oil-finished, textured wood surface, everyday use can gradually develop a richer patina rather than producing the peeling associated with film-forming finishes.
