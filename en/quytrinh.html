Process

Built on careful selection and restraint. We focus on what matters most: durability, practical performance, and the natural beauty of wood.

01

Site Assessment & Consultation

We begin by understanding the needs and context of the space, from its function to everyday patterns of use. Every consultation at CASA is grounded in actual site conditions, helping determine the most suitable board construction and wood surface for each living environment.

02

Installation

Installation is carried out directly and closely controlled at every stage. From site preparation and substrate treatment to placing each board, every detail is carefully calibrated to ensure precision, stability, and a seamless feel across the finished surface in use.

03

Ongoing Care & Support

CASA is committed to ongoing guidance on proper use, periodic maintenance and long-term care of the wood surface. Our goal is to ensure the flooring maintains structural stability, its natural feel, and authentic beauty throughout its service life.
